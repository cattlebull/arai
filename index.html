View Full Catalog
Standard

PLA+

  • Pros: Excellent detail
  • Cons: Low heat resistance (60°C)
  • Best Use: Prototypes, Display models
Industrial

ABS

  • Pros: High impact strength, Acetone smoothable
  • Cons: Shrinkage (less dimensional accuracy)
  • Best Use: Functional parts
Versatile

PETG

  • Pros: Chemical resistant, Ductile
  • Cons: Difficult to post-process
  • Best Use: Snap-fits, Liquid containers
Outdoor

ASA

  • Pros: UV resistant, Weatherproof
  • Cons: Same as ABS
  • Best Use: Outdoor parts, Automotive
