# SEO Strategy for Apex 3D Labs

## Keywords
- **Primary Keywords**: "3D Printing Austin", "Custom 3D Printing Service", "Industrial 3D Printing"
- **Secondary Keywords**: "PLA+ Printing", "ABS Printing", "Rapid Prototyping Austin", "3D Manufacturing"

## Local Intent
The strategy focuses on **hyper-local SEO** for Austin, Texas:
1.  **JSON-LD LocalBusiness Schema**: We've embedded structured data that tells search engines exactly where you are, your phone number, and opening hours. This is crucial for appearing in the "Local Pack" (Google Maps results).
2.  **Geographic Modifiers**: The title tag ("Apex 3D Labs Austin") and content explicitly mention "Austin, TX" and specific regions/streets ("Downtown Austin").
3.  **Google Analytics 4**: A placeholder for GA4 is included to track local traffic sources.

## Conversion
- **Lazy Loading**: Images use native optimizations or lightweight sizes to ensure fast load times (vital for Mobile SEO).
- **Clear CTAs**: "Get a Quote" and "Start Your Project" are placed prominently.
